# Governance for [PROJECT NAME]

[PROJECT NAME] is guided by Love, Efficiency, Resilience, and Openness. Treat
every participant with dignity; harassment, discrimination, dishonesty, and
avoidable harm are not acceptable.

Contributors propose changes, maintainers review routine work, and stewards
resolve unresolved or project-direction decisions. Seek rough consensus first.
For material choices, record the real-world gain, monetary impact, and hybrid
trade-off using the Three Ledgers framework.

Raise conduct or conflict concerns privately with a maintainer where possible.
Stewards will hear affected people, make a proportionate decision, and may
warn, limit participation, or remove access to protect the community.
