# Governance & Code of Conduct — [PROJECT NAME]

[BRIEF DESCRIPTION]

This document describes how decisions are made in this project and the standard of conduct expected of everyone who participates.

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Decision-Making

- **Maintainer(s):** [PROJECT NAME] is currently maintained by [MAINTAINER NAME/ORG].
- **Proposals:** Non-trivial changes should be proposed via a GitHub issue before implementation.
- **Consensus:** Maintainers aim for lazy consensus — if no objection is raised within a reasonable period, a proposal is considered accepted.
- **Final say:** In case of disagreement, the maintainer(s) make the final call, favoring the option that best serves the project's stated goals.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the maintainers via a GitHub issue marked private/sensitive, or through the contact method listed in the repository README. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
