# Governance

Wisdom Scaffold exists to make practical knowledge easier to reuse, especially
for people operating with limited energy, money, connectivity, or hardware.
Its community values are Love, Efficiency, Resilience, and Openness.

## Roles

- **Contributors** report issues, improve documentation, and submit changes.
- **Maintainers** review changes for accuracy, safety, and alignment.
- **Stewards** resolve disagreements and protect the project’s purpose.

## Decisions

Use the [Three Ledgers decision framework](/home/runner/work/wisdom-scaffold/wisdom-scaffold/THREE_LEDGERS_DECISION_FRAMEWORK.md)
for material decisions: record the real-world gain, monetary impact, and
hybrid trade-off. Seek rough consensus; maintainers make routine decisions,
and stewards make unresolved or project-direction decisions.

## Conduct and Conflict Resolution

Treat people with dignity. Do not harass, discriminate, misrepresent work, or
knowingly introduce avoidable harm. Raise concerns privately with a maintainer
when possible. Stewards will listen to affected people, document the decision
appropriately, and may warn, limit participation, or remove access to protect
the community.

## Changes to This Document

Propose governance changes through a pull request that explains the
three-ledger impact. Material changes require a steward approval.
