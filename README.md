# wisdom-scaffold

**A repeatable method for converting hours of work into assets that compound — three ledgers, one scaffold, everything shared.**

Most work evaporates when you finish it. The scaffold treats every piece of work — a certification, a seed crop, a build, a course — as an entry in three ledgers:

1. **Real-world ledger** — durable capability, tools, relationships, seeds, built things. Survives inflation and job loss.
2. **Monetary ledger** — cash flow and ROI on the same effort.
3. **Hybrid decision** — when the two ledgers disagree, the ledger comparison *is* the answer.

## Example decision: certify in seed saving?

Real-world ledger: cost 30 hours study; gain knowledge reusable indefinitely, teaching capability, credibility with farm partnerships, resilience independent of employment.
Monetary ledger: cost ~$400 + opportunity cost; gain premium seed prices, workshop income, consulting rate lift; breakeven in months, not years.
Hybrid decision: pursue it *because* both ledgers are positive — the durable asset is the real prize, the cash is the confirmation.

## Layer model

The scaffold stacks five layers, each feeding the next: **structure** (housing, workshops), **production** (seeds, mushrooms, algae), **growth** (exponential propagation curves — one packet becomes thousands), **authority** (documented skills → certifications → portfolio), **legal structure** (operations now, mission alignment long-term).

## Tools

- `YOUR_PATHWAYS.md` — your personal instance of the scaffold (the only file you edit privately)
- `cathedral-status.sh` — track what's entered, what's missing; optional `LLM_CMD` hook drafts suggestions with a local model; deployment materials in `docs/cathedral-uplift/`

See `cathedral-status.sh --help` for all options.
